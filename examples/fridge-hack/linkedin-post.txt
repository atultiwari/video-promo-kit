𝗡𝗼𝗯𝗼𝗱𝘆 𝘁𝗼𝘂𝗰𝗵𝗲𝗱 𝗮 𝘀𝗶𝗻𝗴𝗹𝗲 𝗳𝗿𝗶𝗱𝗴𝗲. 𝗛𝘂𝗻𝗱𝗿𝗲𝗱𝘀 𝗼𝗳 𝘀𝘂𝗽𝗲𝗿𝗺𝗮𝗿𝗸𝗲𝘁𝘀 𝗹𝗼𝘀𝘁 𝘁𝗵𝗲𝗶𝗿 𝗰𝗼𝗹𝗱 𝗰𝗵𝗮𝗶𝗻 𝗮𝗻𝘆𝘄𝗮𝘆. 🌡️

On the night of 11–12 October, attackers remotely pushed fridges and freezers in hundreds of French supermarkets to 50°C. One store reported 80°C. Food was binned, equipment melted, some stores caught fire.

It wasn't an AI attack.

𝗔𝗻𝗱 𝘁𝗵𝗮𝘁'𝘀 𝗲𝘅𝗮𝗰𝘁𝗹𝘆 𝘄𝗵𝘆 𝗲𝘃𝗲𝗿𝘆 𝗱𝗼𝗰𝘁𝗼𝗿 𝘂𝘀𝗶𝗻𝗴 𝗔𝗜 𝘀𝗵𝗼𝘂𝗹𝗱 𝗽𝗮𝘆 𝗮𝘁𝘁𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻.

Our hospitals and labs used to run on closed networks. AI is quietly connecting them to the open internet: cloud AI tools, vendor remote access, models left open "just for testing".

The supermarket controllers were a networked box, maintained remotely by a vendor, running software nobody updated, trusted without checking.

That's a description of a lot of healthcare AI too.
