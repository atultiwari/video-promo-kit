✅ THE 7-POINT CHECKLIST
1. Nothing administrative faces the open internet. Use a VPN or allow-list.
2. Patch on a schedule. Keep an inventory of everything you run.
3. No default or shared passwords. Ask vendors about support/backdoor accounts.
4. AI reads by default. Any action needs a human click.
5. Monitor independently. Audit AI outputs over time, not just at launch.
6. Log everything, so you know what to recall.
7. Treat vendors and contractors as part of your security.
