Your pulse oximeter isn't AI. The text printed on your ECG might be.
Here's how to tell the difference, and the one question to ask before you trust any medical AI tool.

Episode 1 of "AI in Medicine — Decoded for Doctors": AI literacy for MBBS and PG students and clinicians, with no maths and no coding. ✅ THREE QUESTIONS FOR ANY AI TOOL
1. Who wrote its rules: experts, or the data?
2. If it learned, from which data: which hospitals, machines, patients?
3. Who checks it, and is that person you?
